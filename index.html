I. The Concept

What is the Builder Table?

A gathering space for female professionals to work on their passion projects together — in a warm, supportive environment that feels less like a coworking space and more like a long Sunday afternoon at a friend’s apartment.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or have been quietly developing an idea for a while, this doesn’t have to be a lonely journey. Join the table, share what you’re working on, grab a coffee, and start building together — sometimes interrupted by brunch and a sharing session with feedback and lessons learned.

Pull up a chair. Open the laptop. Order another coffee. Tell us what you’re making — and let’s make it a little less lonely.
— The House Rule

II. How a Sunday Goes

An afternoon, more or less.

Two flavours of session, alternating — one for unfurling, one for the work itself. Come to one, come to both. The chair is yours either way.

No. 01 · The Opening

Introductions & Brunch

First Sunday · 11:00 — 14:30
  • i.Arrivals, coffee, the slow circling of the table
  • ii.Open-table sharing — what are you building, and why now?
  • iii.Early discussion: where you’re stuck, what you’re unsure of
  • iv.A long, generous brunch
  • v.Set intentions for the next stretch of work
No. 02 · The Ongoing

Work, Feedback, Repeat

Following Sundays · 13:00 — 17:00
  • i.Focused work time — heads down, side by side
  • ii.Iteration on what you brought last time
  • iii.Peer feedback in small clusters
  • iv.Lessons learned — short, candid, written down
  • v.Accountability check-ins for the week ahead

III. Where We Meet

Rotating rooms, one long table.

The table moves. Sometimes it’s a real one with too many chairs and too much bread; sometimes it’s a corner of a café with the right kind of light. Hosts rotate, the warmth doesn’t.
